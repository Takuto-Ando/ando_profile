I am Takuto Ando, a first-year master's student at Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST) in the Computer Architecture Laboratory.

I explore AI and image-processing technologies from the perspective of computer architecture, pursuing hardware and software solutions that address real-world challenges. I actively share research outcomes through conference presentations and publications.

At present, I am developing leading-edge AI applications—such as large language models—on the next-generation non-von Neumann accelerator “IMAX” and working to maximize their performance.

I have presented research five times at domestic conferences and six times at international venues.
My primary-authored papers, including international proceedings, total six international and one domestic publication.
Among these, my research at NAIST includes four international publications and two awards.

### Current Focus Areas
* Executing large language models on the non-von Neumann CGRA “IMAX,” optimizing memory access and host cooperation to improve end-to-end performance
* Porting diverse generative AI applications such as Whisper and Stable Diffusion to IMAX, demonstrating power savings through FP16 kernel design and data-flow optimization
* Exploring CGRA architecture scaling from edge to server environments while documenting and disseminating research results through papers and conference talks
